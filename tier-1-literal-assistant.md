# Tier 1 - The Literal Assistant

## Original instruction
"Make me breakfast."

## What a literal machine does not know
1. **Which food?** "Breakfast" is a category, not a dish. The machine does not know whether I mean eggs, bread, cereal, or something else.
2. **How much?** It does not know the number of eggs, slices of bread, or portions.
3. **Where is everything?** It does not know where the ingredients, pan, plate, or stove are.
4. **What equipment to use, and how?** It does not know which tool to use, how to turn it on, or what heat setting to choose.
5. **What order to follow?** It does not know what must happen first (wash hands, heat the pan) and what comes next.
6. **How long, and how do we know it is done?** It has no idea when to stop cooking.
7. **Where does the finished meal go?** It does not know to serve it on a plate or where to put the plate.
8. **What counts as "finished"?** It does not know the task includes serving and reporting back.

## Precise instructions: "Make me breakfast"
**Goal:** Prepare 2 scrambled eggs and 2 slices of toast, and serve them on a plate on the dining table.

**Starting conditions:** Eggs are in the refrigerator, bread is in the bread box on the kitchen counter, the frying pan is in the cupboard under the stove, and the plates are in the cupboard above the sink.

**Steps**
1. Wash your hands with soap and water for 20 seconds. Dry them with a clean towel.
2. Go to the refrigerator and open the door.
3. Take out 2 eggs and a block of butter. Close the door.
4. Put the eggs and butter on the kitchen counter.
5. Take 1 frying pan from the cupboard under the stove. Place it on the front-left burner.
6. Take 1 mixing bowl and 1 fork from the cupboard and put them on the counter.
7. Crack both eggs into the bowl. Remove any shell pieces.
8. Beat the eggs with the fork for 30 seconds, until the yolk and white are fully mixed with no streaks.
9. Add one pinch of salt to the bowl and mix once.
10. Take 2 slices of bread from the bread box and place them in the toaster.
11. Press the toaster lever down and set the dial to medium.
12. Turn the front-left burner to medium heat.
13. Add 1 teaspoon of butter to the pan and wait until it has melted completely.
14. Pour the beaten eggs into the pan.
15. Stir slowly with a spatula every 10 seconds.
16. **Check:** The eggs are done when no liquid egg is visible and they look firm and slightly moist. This usually takes 2 to 3 minutes.
17. Turn the burner **off**.
18. When the toast pops up, take it out. **Check:** Both slices are golden brown, not black. If not, toast again for 30 seconds.
19. Take 1 plate from the cupboard above the sink and place it on the counter.
20. Put the toast on the plate, then spoon the eggs next to it.
21. Carry the plate to the dining table and place it in front of the person's seat.
22. Place the used pan, bowl, fork, and spatula in the sink.
23. Tell the person: "Breakfast is ready."

**The task is finished when:** the plate is on the table, the burner is off, and the person has been told.

## Reflection
**Which parts of my original instruction required me to make assumptions?**

- **"Breakfast"**: I assumed a specific meal (eggs and toast). The word itself names no food.
- **"Make"**: I assumed it means cooking, serving, and notifying, not only cooking.
- **"Me"**: I assumed where the person is and where they want to eat.
- **Everything unspoken**: ingredients, quantity, location of items, equipment, heat level, timing, and the definition of "done".

**Key lesson:** Humans fill in missing information automatically from context and experience. A computer cannot. Every detail must be written into the instructions, or the computer will fail or do something unexpected.
